# Name the people in your photos

Name the faces in your photos, so People shows each person's photos and Lifestream can tell you
who was there. This guide also joins one person's numbers, emails and face on **People**, adds the
other names they are called, and fixes a match that went to the wrong person.

## Before you start

- Photos in Lifestream, from [Photos](./connectors/photos.md),
  [Google Photos](./connectors/google-photos.md) or [Camera](./connectors/camera.md).
- The Mac app. You name faces in its window. The iPhone app edits a person, but does not name
  faces.

## Turn on faces

If you left **Recognise people in your photos** on during setup, faces is on already. Go to
[Name a face](#name-a-face).

1. Open **People**, and click **Turn on faces** at the side.
2. In the **Faces** window, under **To connect**, click **Install** beside **Get the face models**.

Lifestream downloads InsightFace's face model from InsightFace's own site, then finds the faces in
your photos and in the pictures of your WhatsApp and iMessage chats. It finds them on your Mac,
and the faces it finds stay there. If more than 500 photos are waiting, it scans them only while
your Mac is plugged in. To scan on battery, click **Scan now** in the same window.

Once faces is on, **Faces settings** at the top of **People** opens the same window. Its **Faces**
switch turns faces off.

## Name a face

Lifestream puts the faces that look like one person in a group. You name each group once.

1. Open **People**, and click **Faces** at the side. Its line says how many groups wait, such as
   "12 to name".
2. Look at the faces on the left. If one is not the same person, click it to take it out.
3. Under **Who is this?**, type a name.
4. Click the person in the list, or press Return for the one that is lit. If they are not in People
   yet, choose **New person** with the name you typed.

The next group comes up. Each answer is saved at once. **Skip** leaves a group for later, and
**Set aside** marks a face you will not name, or that is not a face. Once you have answered every
group in 10 or more photos, Faces stops to ask. Click **Keep going** to name the smaller groups, or
**Done**.

New photos of a person you named are named as they arrive. If a model describes your photos, it
sees the names you gave: [Photos](./connectors/photos.md#what-a-model-reads) says what it reads.

### Check the faces Lifestream is not sure of

Faces also asks **Is this** *a name*?, with faces that may be that person. Click each face that is
not them. Then choose **All** *a number* **are** *the name*, or **Add** *a number* **to** *the
name* once you have clicked some. If none of them is that person, choose **None of these are**
*the name*.

## Join one person across messages, contacts and photos

Lifestream joins the numbers and emails on one contact card into one person. When it is not sure
that two of them are the same person, it suggests it.

1. Open **People**, and click the person.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Under **Numbers and emails**, click **Add** on each suggestion that is theirs, such as a row
   that says **Same person?** An added suggestion joins the person when you save. Click its tick to
   take it back.
4. To add something it did not suggest, type a number, an email or a name in **Add a number,
   email, link, person or face**, and press Return.
5. If they have no face yet, click **Mark their face**, then click their face.
6. Click **Save**.

**Organise your people**, at the side of **People**, asks the same about each person you talk to
often, one at a time. On your iPhone, tap **Organise** in **People** when it shows a count.

On your iPhone, open **People**, tap the person, then tap **Edit**. The same suggestions are under
**Numbers and emails**: tap **Add** on each one that is them, type in **Add a number, email or link**
to add another, then tap **Save**.

## Add the other names a person is called

**Also called** holds a person's other names, such as a nickname. Lifestream fills it from the
nicknames on their contact cards. Search finds a person by each of these names.

1. Open the person, and click **Edit**.
2. Under **Also called**, click **Add a name**, type the name, and press Return. To take a name off,
   click × beside it.
3. Click **Save**.

A person has at most 12 other names. A name you take off stays off, even if their contact card
still holds it. On your iPhone, tap the **Also called** row in **Edit**, then swipe a name to
remove it.

## Your own page

**You** is the one person on **People** who is you. Lifestream makes You when you say your name
during setup, and your own WhatsApp, iMessage and Gmail accounts join You by themselves. The **You**
card at the side of **People** opens your page, with your numbers, emails and photos. You are not
in the list of people or its counts, but search finds you.

To add more of yourself, such as a second email or another number, open your page, click **Edit**,
and use **Add a number, email, link or person** under **Yours**. Suggestions that may be yours
show there too, each with its tick. A person you pick there joins you. No other person's **Edit**
offers you. Your own accounts stay yours: they have no ×.

In **Faces**, the first group that looks like you has a **You** row. Choose it to name your
face.

## Fix a wrong match

- **A face in a group is someone else:** click it before you name the group.
- **You named a group wrong:** click **Back**, or press ⌘Z, before you leave Faces.
- **A number or an email is someone else's:** in **Edit**, click × beside it, then click **Save**.
- **Two people were joined by mistake:** in **Edit**, find the person under **Joined in**, click ×
  beside them, then click **Save**. They are split off and go back as they were, with their
  numbers, emails, faces and photos. On your iPhone, swipe the row and tap **Split off**. You can
  split someone off at any time, not only right after you joined them.
- **A suggestion is wrong:** in **Edit**, clear its tick before you click **Save**. In **Organise
  your people**, click **Undo** after **Confirm** to take the answer back.
