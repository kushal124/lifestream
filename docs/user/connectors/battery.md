# Battery

The Battery connector records what the iPhone app costs your iPhone's battery: a record of what
Lifestream itself uses. The iPhone app records it and sends it to Lifestream.

## What it brings in

- What each part of the iPhone app's work costs, such as Location, Photos or sending to Lifestream,
  in windows of up to about 15 minutes.
- Your iPhone's battery level, whether it is charging, Low Power Mode, how warm it is and whether
  Background App Refresh is on, each time one of them changes. These are the whole iPhone's.
- The report iOS makes about the iPhone app each day: its use of the processor, graphics,
  location, network and screen.
- The crash reports iOS makes about the iPhone app.

## Before you start

- The iPhone app, [paired with your Mac](../pair-your-iphone.md).

## Add Battery

Battery needs no step and no permission. It starts once your iPhone is paired. It is not listed on
**Connectors**.

## Where it shows up

Battery has no rows on Timeline, People or Places.

## What a model reads

No AI model reads Battery.

## Settings

Battery has no settings. Your iPhone decides when to send.
