# Lifestream notes

The Lifestream notes connector brings the notes you write in Lifestream on your iPhone into
Lifestream, with the photos you take in the app ([Camera](./camera.md)), in one row. Your iPhone sends
each note after you change it.

The connector named Notes reads Apple's Notes app on your Mac: see [Notes](./notes.md).

## What it brings in

- Every note you write in Lifestream on your iPhone: its title, its text, its formatting, its
  folder, and whether it is pinned.
- The photos and files you attach to a note.
- Every folder, with its name.
- The notes you move to **Recently deleted**.

A note leaves **Recently deleted** after 30 days, or when you delete it there for good. Lifestream
then marks it as deleted.

You can write notes before you pair your iPhone. Your iPhone sends them once it is paired. If you
install the iPhone app again and pair it with the same Mac, your notes and folders come back from
Lifestream.

## Before you start

- Lifestream on your iPhone, [paired with your Mac](../pair-your-iphone.md). Lifestream notes
  needs no permission.

## Write a note

There is nothing to add. To write a note, tap **+** at the top of Timeline, then **Note**. To see
all your notes, tap **+**, then **All notes**. You can also open **Connectors**, tap **Lifestream
notes**, then tap **Your notes**.

To attach something, tap the paperclip above the keyboard, then **Photo library**, **Take photo**
or **Choose file**.

## Where it shows up

Timeline and Search do not show these notes. **Chat** can read them when a question needs them.
To find a note on your iPhone, use the search field at the top of your folders.

## What a model reads

No AI model reads Lifestream notes, so its window has no **AI** section.

When Chat or an agent answers a question, it can read this connector's data too. [What leaves
your Mac](../what-leaves-your-mac.md) says what leaves your Mac then, and to whom.

## Settings

Lifestream notes has none of the [settings every connector
has](./README.md#settings-every-connector-has): your iPhone sends each note when you change it, and
there is no past to bring in.
