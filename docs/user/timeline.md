# Timeline

Timeline shows your life one day at a time: where you were, and what happened there. Chat, Search
and People open it when you follow something back to its moment.

## How a day is laid out

A day is a list of where you were, from morning to night. Each stay is a place you spent time at,
such as **Home** or your office, with its start time and how long you stayed. Between two stays is
the move between them. Inside each stay is what happened there: messages, calls, emails, photos,
music, workouts and your time at the Mac.

A Tuesday, for example, might show a stay at **Home** until 8:40, a move to the office, then the
office stay with a call, 3 photos and messages with Person A.

A day starts at 4:00 in the morning, so a late night stays with the day it began. A day can still
change later: when a connector brings in your past, Lifestream adds it to the days it belongs to.

Stays and moves come from your iPhone's [Location](./connectors/location.md), your photos and
[Google Maps Timeline](./connectors/google-timeline.md). Until a place has a name, Timeline says what
it is near, such as **Near Home**, or its city.

## Read a day on your Mac

1. Click **Timeline** in the sidebar. It opens today.
2. Click a stay to open or fold what happened there.
3. Click a message, an email, a photo or any other row to open it in a panel over Timeline. Press
   Esc to close the panel.

The side column beside the day shows a map of the day, and the day's **Places** and **People**. In
a narrow window, **People** and **Places** are buttons at the top instead.

To name a place you stayed at, click **Name it** beside the stay. Places opens with that spot, so
you can name it. See [Name your places](./name-your-places.md).

### Move between days

- Click **Previous day** or **Next day** at the top, or press ⌘← or ⌘→.
- Click **Today**, or press T, to go back to today.
- Click the date at the top to choose a day.

### Keys on Timeline

| Press | To |
|---|---|
| ↑ or ↓ | Move from row to row. |
| → or Space | Open a stay. |
| ← | Fold a stay. |
| Return | Open the row you are on. |
| ⌥→ or ⌥← | Open or fold every stay of the day. |
| ⌘← or ⌘→ | Go to the day before or after. |
| T | Go to today. |

## Read a day on your iPhone

1. Tap the **Timeline** tab. It opens today.
2. Swipe left or right to move to the day before or after.
3. To jump to another day, tap the date at the top. It lists every day, month by month. Tap a day, or
   tap **Today**.
4. Tap a stay to open it, and tap a row to see it whole.

Timeline's **+**, at the top right, adds a voice note, a photo, a note or a check-in to your day.
See [The iPhone app](./iphone-app.md#the-buttons-at-the-top).

## When a day looks empty

A day with nothing in it says **Nothing recorded this day.** Right after setup, older days fill in
last, because each connector brings in your past newest first. Photo descriptions, voice notes and
summaries of your time at the screen come only once an AI model reads them.
[Timeline is empty or thin](./problems.md#timeline-is-empty-or-thin) lists every cause.
