# The iPhone app

The iPhone app does two jobs. It records where you go, how you move, your health, your photos and
more, and sends it to Lifestream on your Mac. It also shows your life as the Lifestream window
does, read from Lifestream on your Mac.

[Pair your iPhone](./pair-your-iphone.md) says how to install and pair it, and what it records.

## The five tabs

The tab bar at the bottom holds five tabs:

| Tab | What it shows | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Chat** | Answers to your questions about your life, as on the Mac. | [Chat on your iPhone](./chat.md#chat-on-your-iphone) |
| **Timeline** | One day at a time. | [Timeline](./timeline.md) |
| **People** | The people in your life. | [Name the people in your photos](./name-people.md) |
| **Places** | The places you go, and the ones that wait for a name. | [Name your places](./name-your-places.md) |
| **Agents** | The AI apps you let read Lifestream, what each may read, and what each asks for. Each app's guide walks you through connecting it. | [Connect an agent](./connect-an-agent.md) |

Until your iPhone is paired, each tab says **Your life shows here once this iPhone is paired with
Lifestream.** Tap **Pair with Lifestream**.

## The buttons at the top

Each tab has the same buttons at its top right:

- **The magnifier** opens Search. See [Search your life](./search.md#search-on-your-iphone).
- **The Connectors button** opens Connectors. A dot on it shows that something needs you, such as
  a connector that needs a permission or an agent that asks for more.

On **Timeline**, a **+** beside them opens what you can add to your day:

- **Voice note** starts a voice note.
- **Photo** opens the camera. See [Camera](./connectors/camera.md).
- **Note** starts a note. See [Lifestream notes](./connectors/iphone-notes.md).
- **Check-in** asks short questions about how you feel. See [Check-ins](./connectors/check-ins.md).
- **All notes** opens the folders of your notes.

On **Chat**, the button at the top left opens your past conversations. Inside a conversation, the
top right holds **New conversation** instead.

## Connectors on your iPhone

**Connectors** lists every connector, as on the Mac, with a line that says how each is doing. Tap
one to open its window. [Connectors](./connectors/README.md) says what every connector shares.

Under **Devices**:

- **Lifestream** says when your iPhone last reached Lifestream on your Mac. Tap it to see your Mac,
  your Apple ID and the last send. There, **Send now** sends what waits at once, **Unpair this
  iPhone** stops it sending, and **Build an export** makes a copy of everything this iPhone has
  recorded.
- **This iPhone** shows its recording status under **Status**, and its version under **About**.
- Your other devices, such as your Mac, each show a line of how they are doing.

## The lock

If your iPhone has a passcode, the iPhone app locks each time you leave it. When you come back, it
asks for Face ID or your passcode before it shows your life again. If you cancel,
it says **Lifestream is locked**. Tap **Unlock** to try again.

The lock covers what the app shows. Recording goes on while the app is locked or closed.

## When your iPhone can't reach your Mac

The iPhone app reads your life from Lifestream on your Mac, so it needs to reach it: on the same
Wi-Fi, or through Tailscale. While it cannot, a strip at the top of each tab says **Can't reach
Lifestream**, and your iPhone keeps what it records until it can send it. [Reach Lifestream when you
are away](./reach-from-anywhere.md) says how to reach it from anywhere.
