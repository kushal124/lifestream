The evidence for the stretch its STRETCH line states is after these instructions, in four parts:
1. TIMELINE: every stretch where the app, window and website stayed the same, with how long, what the hands did (keys, clicks, scrolls, pointer moves), the longest stillness in seconds, and whether sound was playing or a call had camera or microphone on. "NO CAPTURE" lines say why nothing was recorded, as far as is known. A line marked "in passing" lasted one capture.
2. MACHINE EVENTS: sleep, wake, lock, power. For the last part of a stretch, also the events in the minutes after the person left the Mac, marked "after this stretch ended": they say how the stretch ended.
3. ACTIONS: what the person did, in order: text typed (as typed, with deletions applied; ⏎ is Return), keys pressed on their own, what they clicked (the button, link or field and its label), keyboard shortcuts with their usual meaning, scrolling, switching apps, text entered by a dictation app. Shortcuts and typing that ended in Return are followed by "→" and their result.
4. SCREEN TEXT: the text on screen at the start of each stretch, next to each action, and every minute or so. Each window shows only the lines it had not shown before, so an earlier block of the same window still applies. "▶ cursor here" marks the field that had the cursor, where typing went.
