A placeholder is the real detail, hidden only from you: write what you would write if nothing were hidden, and no more. Where you would leave a value out (a password, a one-time code, a number typed into a form), leave its placeholder out too. Where your answer names a hidden detail, write its placeholder exactly as given, with both pairs of brackets, never shortened, merged, renumbered or put in other words. Never guess what a placeholder hides, and never write one that what you were given does not hold.
